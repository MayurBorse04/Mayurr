# Mayurr This Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) report details an event involving a 36-year-old female patient (initials A.V., weight 47 kg) who experienced severe abdominal pain following medication intake for an acute exacerbation of gout. The patient was prescribed oral Ibuprofen (400 mg twice daily) and Febuxostat (80 mg), starting on July 27, 2026. Following administration, she presented with severe abdominal pain lasting three days. The suspected offending medication was identified as Ibuprofen 400 mg. After Ibuprofen was discontinued on July 28, 2026, the patient's complaints began to improve and completely resolved by July 29, 2026. The reaction was categorized as non-serious, and the Causality Assessment noted on the document references Pregabalin.
